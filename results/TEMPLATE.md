## Experiment card

- **Question:** what we want to learn, in one sentence.
- **Cheapest decisive test:** what is run, on what, and why nothing cheaper would answer it.
- **Baseline:** what it is compared with (a trivial method, or the current best, on the same test).
- **Deciding metric:** the one number that decides; the checkpoint is selected by the same metric on validation.
- **What would change the next step:** the result that would make us stop, switch, or continue.

## Datasheet

- **Sources and licenses:** each source, its license, and what it was used for.
- **Splits:** groups (`dianoia.splits`), manifest checksum, units per split and medium.
- **Filters:** what was removed and why (bots, length, content).
- **Audit:** `audit-data.py` result (pass, numbers, warnings), audit file checksum.
- **Known problems:** anything a reader should discount the result for.
