# Phase 14 pair rubric for papers (fixed 10/08/2026, before any reading)

Phase 10's rubric (`results/phase-10-analogy/RUBRIC.md`) with its labels reworded for papers. The CITING paper is a
business, management or accounting paper; the CITED paper is outside the Social Sciences. The why, the gate (C and G
count as real) and the consistency rule are phase 10's, unchanged.

- **C, analogical borrowing.** The cited field's own way of handling its own problem is carried into a management
  problem, and seeing it required noticing that the two problems are alike; the roles map onto different things.
- **G, general method used as is.** A statistical test, a standard algorithm, a measurement instrument or a technology,
  generic and named the same way in both, doing the same job; the cited field is incidental.
- **A, same topic.** Both study the same kind of thing (for example hospital operations, or the energy use of
  buildings), even though they are filed under different fields.
- **B, unrelated.** Nothing specific is reused: a background citation, or only shared generic words ("model").

The test question: could the citing author have picked up the reused thing from a general textbook or a software
manual, without looking at the cited field? Yes → G (or A if both study the same kind of thing). No → C.

Readings are blind (numbers only), each with the 10 gold pairs shuffled in. Under 9/10 agreement (C and G as one) the
reading does not count. Sonnet's prompts v3p and v4p (`scripts/p10-label.py`) carry the same wording, with invented
examples.
